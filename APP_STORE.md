## Description

See where the planets are, right now.

Starpath is a live linear zodiac chart that shows the real-time positions of the Sun, Moon, and planets along the ecliptic. No horoscopes, no predictions — just the actual sky, beautifully rendered.

Swipe through time to watch planetary movements unfold. See Mercury race ahead, Saturn barely move, and Venus drift between evening and morning star. Tap any planet for its position, speed, and distance.

Features:
- Real-time positions of Sun, Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, and Neptune
- Time travel — scroll days, months, or years to explore past and future skies
- Drift curves showing planetary trajectories over time
- Dynamic moon phase icon reflecting the actual lunar phase
- Rotating star sphere background
- Home screen widget with current planetary layout
- Control Center widget showing current Sun sign
- Minimal, distraction-free interface

Built with astronomical precision using classical orbital mechanics. No internet connection required.
